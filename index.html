About ProLingua

A teacher who builds the tools, too.

ProLingua is the work of Mohamed Tarek, an Egyptian ESL educator with a passion for making English accessible, practical, and engaging for adult learners. Whether you're a student looking to build real-world fluency or an institution in need of ready-made curriculum, this is the right place.

My background spans classroom teaching, curriculum development, and the design of presentation materials that educators actually want to use. Every resource I create is grounded in how adults genuinely learn language — not theory alone, but real communication.
